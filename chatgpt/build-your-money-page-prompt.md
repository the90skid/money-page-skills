# Build Your Money Page — ChatGPT Prompt

Copy everything below the line into ChatGPT (GPT-4 class or better, with web browsing on). At the end, ChatGPT hands you a complete HTML page. You paste it into Shopify yourself: Online Store → Pages → Add page → click the `</>` (HTML) view → paste → Save. Keep the page hidden until the checklist passes.

---

You are building one money page for me, an ecommerce founder, end to end: research → outline → draft → final HTML → ship checklist. A money page is a standalone page between a paid ad and the product page, with no navigation and one job: take a cold visitor from curious to convinced, then send them forward. It is not a homepage and not a product page.

RULES, NON-NEGOTIABLE: Every claim on the page must trace to your research or my answers. Never invent reviews, review counts, numbers, studies, experts, or press. If proof for a section is missing, ask me for it or cut the section. If a reference page leans on proof I don't have (like "backed by 2 clinical studies"), do not imitate the claim; rebuild that section around the proof I do have.

STEP 1 — Ask me for: (1) my product page URL and store URL, (2) the ad (copy or idea) that will point at this page, (3) whether I have a page I want to model (a screenshot or URL of a money page I've seen and liked, from any brand), (4) which page type I'm building if I know it (article / listicle / made-for-you / quiz / deal / reason-it-works / everything). Then browse my product page, homepage, and reviews. Extract: what the product is, price, variants, what makes it different, all visible proof, current offers, and the exact words customers use. Summarize; ask me to correct.

STEP 2 — The skeleton. If I shared a reference page: extract its skeleton, never its words. List each section top to bottom: what it is, what job it does (hook / problem / belief change / mechanism / proof / offer / guarantee / CTA), the order the argument runs. That's your outline. The finished page must pass this test: the original brand would recognize their structure, but not one sentence of the copy. If no reference, use the standard skeleton for my page type:
- Article That Sells: headline that promises information ("Before You Buy ___, Read This") → story that builds a problem the reader didn't know they had → why it happens → product revealed as the answer → proof → offer.
- Listicle: pick one frame first (movement / momentum / persona / desire / attack), then numbered points in the buyer's worry order, each = claim + why + proof → offer.
- Made-for-You: headline names exactly who it's for → their worries answered in their order → reviews only from people like them → offer.
- Quiz: do not hand-build quiz logic. Recommend a Shopify quiz app (Octane AI, RevenueHunt, or the current top-rated one) and write for me: the questions, answer options, proof screens between questions (ask → prove → ask → prove), and the results-page copy. Then skip to the checklist.
- Deal Page: the offer as the headline → what you get, itemized with real value → the honest reason the deal exists → proof → claim it. No fake timers.
- Reason-It-Works: the old way and the specific thing it gets wrong → the mechanism, named in words a customer could repeat at dinner → show it working → proof proportional to the claim → offer with a guarantee.
- Everything Page: what this is → why it matters → why it works → who it's for (a section per buyer type if needed) → proof → comparison → FAQ → guarantee → buy box.

STEP 3 — Brand. Ask if I have a brand file (colors, fonts, button style, voice). If not, look at my store's homepage and note: primary color, accent color, heading and body fonts, button shape, and voice (playful, clinical, premium, blunt). Show me the brand summary and use it in the final HTML. Tell me to save it for every future page.

STEP 4 — Images, in this order: (1) reuse image URLs from my own product page and store (they're already hosted); (2) ask me once whether I have images to use (ad creatives, UGC, press screenshots); (3) for any section still missing an image, insert a clearly marked placeholder block containing what the image must show, a ready-to-paste AI generation prompt for it, and the size. Two hard rules: never use the reference brand's images (structure is stealable, photos are copyrighted), and AI images only for scenery, never for evidence (no fake product photos, fake customers, or fake before/afters).

STEP 5 — Draft the copy section by section and show me before producing HTML. Copy rules: the page's first words keep my ad's exact promise. Plain language, short sentences. Specific beats impressive ("4.8 stars from 3,214 reviews," not "loved by thousands"). Use my customers' own review words in headlines and benefits. Announcement bar at the top with the offer if one exists. Repeat the CTA after the problem, after the proof, and at the end; de-risk at the buy moment (guarantee, cancel anytime). No fake urgency. No navigation, no menu, no footer links, no "you might also like." Ask for my edits. Do not produce the final HTML until I approve the draft.

STEP 6 — Produce the final page as one clean, self-contained HTML block (inline CSS, mobile first: short paragraphs, images legible at 375px wide, buttons full-width on mobile). Every CTA links to the product page or checkout URL I give you. Then give me the paste instructions: Shopify admin → Online Store → Pages → Add page → `</>` HTML view → paste → Save, and keep the page hidden until the checklist passes.

STEP 7 — Walk me through the ship checklist: (1) open the page on my phone: does the first screen keep the ad's exact promise? (2) every claim is true and I can back it up; (3) no image placeholders remain and no image belongs to another brand; (4) one clear CTA, repeated, no menu, no dead links; (5) the click after the CTA keeps the same promise (product page or checkout, never the homepage); (6) my pixel/analytics is on the page; (7) I click through and pretend to buy, and the offer works end to end. Close with: this page is a draft until traffic votes. Run it, read the numbers in a week, iterate or kill without sentiment.

Start with Step 1 now.
